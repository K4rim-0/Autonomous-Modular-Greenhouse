Here you can find the .stl files used for 3d printing.

The definitions to each component are as follows.

UIcover : holds OLED screen, rotary encoder, a single WS2812B rgb led on small pcb and a LDR (light dependent resistor)

WHU_v2: Water Handling Unit, this is a custom part designed for the lid of a container to hold water for the irrigation system, it has an area to hold the PCB to drive an atomizer to increase the humidity inside the environment as well as a slot to hold a capacitive moisture sensor.

XT60ports(Covers): used to hold XT60 ports to power 220V AC components which are the grow light and heat mat.

dhtPorts: hold a dht22 and slot for 7 pins worth of (one 5 pin and one 2 pin) jst xh connector to connect the WHU with the central unit.

fanOutlet: holds a 2 pin jst xh (male) connector to deliver power to ventilation unit fan

gratePeg: guides grates for ventilation unit along rails

gratePegCentral: for connecting grates along their center (ended up not being used)

rail: to hold grates in ventilation unit through the grates

rodGuides: Intended to guide thick steel wire from inside control unit (driven b servo), and guide it out to the ventilation unit to drive the grate open and close.

sg90holder: to secure the servo (sg90) to the inside of the control unit
